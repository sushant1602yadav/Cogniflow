
COGNIFLOW CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY

1. Refund Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a refund if:
- The product was delivered in a damaged condition.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- The order was cancelled before shipment.
- The product was returned within the permitted return period.

2. Refund Time
- Refund requests are reviewed within 2 business days.
- Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
- The actual credit time depends on the customer's bank or payment provider.

3. Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds are generally not available if:
- The product was damaged by the customer after delivery.
- The return request was submitted after the permitted return period.
- The product is marked as non-returnable.

4. Missing Refund
If a customer has not received an approved refund:
- First verify the refund approval status.
- Check the transaction reference.
- Ask the customer to contact their bank if the processing period has passed.
- Escalate unresolved cases to the finance team.

5. Refund Approval
- Refund requests must be reviewed by the support team.
- Refunds above ₹10,000 require manager approval.
- AI systems can recommend refunds but cannot approve or execute them independently.

6. Customer Communication
Support representatives must clearly explain:
- Whether the refund is eligible.
- The expected processing time.
- Any additional information required from the customer.
