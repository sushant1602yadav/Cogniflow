
COGNIFLOW CUSTOMER DELIVERY POLICY

1. Standard Delivery
- Standard delivery usually takes 3-7 business days.
- Delivery timelines may vary depending on the customer's location.
- Customers receive an order confirmation after successful payment.

2. Delayed Delivery
- If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery period, the customer can raise a support request.
- Support representatives must check the shipment tracking status.
- If tracking information has not changed for 48 hours, the case should be escalated to the logistics team.

3. Damaged Delivery
- Customers should report damaged products within 48 hours of delivery.
- Customers should provide photographs of the damaged product and packaging.
- Eligible damaged-delivery cases may qualify for a replacement or refund.

4. Failed Delivery
- If delivery fails because the customer is unavailable, another delivery attempt may be scheduled.
- If the delivery address is incorrect, the customer must contact support.
- Repeated delivery failures should be escalated to the logistics team.

5. Delivery Escalation
- Delivery issues unresolved for more than 5 business days must be escalated.
- High-priority delivery complaints should receive immediate attention.

6. AI and Human Approval
- AI can analyze delivery complaints and recommend actions.
- AI cannot independently issue refunds or authorize compensation.
- Sensitive actions require human approval.
