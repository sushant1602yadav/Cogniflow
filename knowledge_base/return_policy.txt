
COGNIFLOW CUSTOMER RETURN POLICY

1. Return Eligibility
- Customers can request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- Products must be unused and returned with original packaging.
- Products must include all accessories and documentation.
- Damaged or incorrect products may qualify for an exception.

2. Non-Returnable Products
- Products marked as non-returnable cannot normally be returned.
- Personalised products are not eligible for standard returns.
- Products damaged through customer misuse are not eligible.

3. Return Request Process
- Customers must provide their order ID.
- Customers must explain the reason for the return.
- Photographs may be requested for damaged or incorrect products.
- Support representatives must verify the purchase and delivery details.

4. Return Inspection
- Returned products are inspected after receipt.
- Approved returns proceed to refund or replacement processing.
- Rejected returns must include a clear explanation.

5. Replacement Policy
- Replacement may be offered for damaged or incorrect products.
- Replacement depends on product availability.
- If replacement is unavailable, an eligible refund may be considered.

6. Escalation
- Return requests unresolved for more than 3 business days should be escalated.
- Disputed return decisions require supervisor review.

7. AI and Human Approval
- AI can check return eligibility using company policies.
- AI can recommend a refund or replacement.
- AI cannot independently approve or execute refunds.
- Requests requiring exceptions must be reviewed by a human representative.
